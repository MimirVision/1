Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Lifted ("the app") is made by [COMPANY NAME] ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

  • No account is required. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • The health information you enter (medications, doses, injection sites, weight, side effects, nutrition) is stored only on your device. We never receive it.
  • We don't use advertising SDKs, we don't track you across apps or websites, and we don't sell data.

Information stored on your device

Everything you log in Lifted is saved in the app's local storage on your iPhone. It is included in your device backups according to your iOS settings. Deleting the app, or using You → Delete all data, permanently erases it from the device.

Information you choose to share

If you export a CSV, create a doctor report PDF or share a progress image, the file is created on your device and sent only where you choose (for example Messages, Mail or Files). We never see these files.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to validate purchases and manage your subscription status. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier, your purchase history and basic device information needed to do this. It does not receive your health information. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We don't operate a push-notification server.

Children

Lifted is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Because your health data never leaves your device, you control it directly: you can view, export and delete it in the app at any time. For questions about purchase records held by RevenueCat or Apple, contact us and we'll help.

Medical disclaimer

Lifted is a tracking and education tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or give dosing advice.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for material changes, notify you in the app.
